Executive summary

Over Q2 we investigated 14 incidents, 3 of which began with credential guessing. Average dwell time before detection was 19 hours, which the board found unacceptable.

Proposed control: escalate whenever an account records at least 7 failed sign-ins within one hour and then signs in successfully. We estimate this would have caught all three incidents; expected alert volume is under two per week.

Budget impact: none (existing SIEM licence).
