# Password Spraying From RDP-EDGE-03

Summary

RDP-EDGE-03 recorded failed sign-ins against many different accounts in a short period. Over roughly 14 minutes the host touched 9 different accounts, including unovak and amir3; none of them went on to a successful login. The affected subnet is 10.128.7.0/24.

Detection follow-up

Flag a source host once failed logins span 8 or more distinct accounts within 15 minutes; repeated failures on a single account do not count. To evaluate this, the log has to provide the username, the login result, the time of each attempt and the host the request originated from.
