The detection flags any successful login lacking MFA on an MFA‑required account, as illustrated by the case of bianca.brennan. Policy mandates that bianca.brennan use multi‑factor authentication, yet the authentication log from BASTION-14 shows a sign‑in where the fields “account_id”, “event_type”, “mfa_used” and “mfa_required” indicate that MFA was not applied. The incident was recorded in ticket INC-68296 and assigned to S. Whitfield for investigation.

The rule extracts the four required fields—“account_id”, “event_type”, “mfa_used”, “mfa_required”—directly from the authentication log to identify the violation. Although the log also contains an “auth_method” attribute, it is explicitly excluded from the rule because it does not affect the detection of this behavior. This concise set of criteria enables rapid identification of MFA bypass attempts on accounts where “mfa_required” is true.
