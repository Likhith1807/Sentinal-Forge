**Ticket INC-62879 – Severity: high – Analyst: K. Sorensen** – Policy dictates that the service account svc-notify22 must authenticate only with a password. The sensor on BASTION-04 recorded a successful login for svc-notify22 that used a certificate instead, breaking the defined authentication baseline. This deviation triggered the investigation, noting that earlier the same day the account completed six routine successful logins without incident.

The detection logic requires log entries that contain the fields “account_id”, “event_type”, “auth_method” and “expected_auth_method”. An alert should fire on any successful login where the “auth_method” does not match the “expected_auth_method”, regardless of direction. The field “mfa_used” was observed to vary between events and is explicitly excluded from the rule, so the pattern must not depend on it.
