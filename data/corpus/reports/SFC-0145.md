# Interactive Authentication by svc-index05

- **Report ID:** SFC-0145
- **Source:** Synthetic corpus report (family F-B4-013)
- **ATT&CK Technique:** T1078.003 (Valid Accounts: Local Accounts)

## Narrative

Per policy, svc-index05 should only ever authenticate with a certificate. On WIN-LAB-17, svc-index05 signed in successfully with password, which does not match its provisioning. Earlier the same day the account had 5 unremarkable successful logins.

## Recommended detection

- The rule should fire when a successful sign-in uses a different authentication method than the account's expected one.
- The rule reads `account_id`, `event_type`, `auth_method` and `expected_auth_method` from the authentication log.
