**Runbook: Flag Unexpected Authentication Method**

**Step one** – Query the authentication logs for the fields "account_id", "event_type", "auth_method", and "expected_auth_method".  

**Step two** – Filter the results to retain only records where "event_type" shows a successful login and "auth_method" does not match "expected_auth_method".  

**Step three** – When a mismatch is detected, open an incident (e.g., INC-42860) and alert the responsible engineer (S. Whitfield) for investigation.  

**Step four** – Record the host where the anomaly occurred (JUMP-19) and verify that the service account svc-etl21 remains provisioned for token authentication only.  

**Step five** – After confirming remediation, close the incident and update any documentation to reflect the corrected authentication expectations.
