# Credential Guessing Against svc-deploy04

- **Report ID:** SFC-0009
- **Source:** Synthetic corpus report (family F-B1-005)
- **ATT&CK Technique:** T1110 (Brute Force), T1078 (Valid Accounts)

## Narrative

Analysts reviewing SSO-GW-06 authentication logs found guessing activity aimed at svc-deploy04. After 9 rejected attempts spread across 73 seconds, svc-deploy04 logged in. Earlier the same day the account had 8 unremarkable successful logins.

## Analyst-confirmed detection parameters

- Detection guidance: raise an alert when 5 or more failed sign-ins for the same account occur within 2 minutes, followed by a successful sign-in for that account.
- Evaluating the rule requires the username, whether each attempt succeeded or failed and event timestamps.
- For scoping, the client IP is not relevant to the behaviour and should be left out of the rule.
