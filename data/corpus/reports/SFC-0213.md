# Unexpected Addition to Domain Admins

Summary

Directory auditing recorded zoe5 being added to a privileged group outside change control. The change came from a workstation at JUMP-08 and 61 minutes later the account was used. Ticket INC-79066 was opened at 22:04 UTC and assigned to S. Whitfield.

Detection follow-up

Alert when any user is added to the Domain Admins group outside an approved change window. Evaluating the rule requires the group being modified and the account that was added.
