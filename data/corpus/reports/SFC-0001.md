# Credential Guessing Against wen.novak

- **Report ID:** SFC-0001
- **Source:** Synthetic corpus report (family F-B1-001)
- **ATT&CK Technique:** T1110 (Brute Force), T1078 (Valid Accounts)

## Narrative

The identity team reported a burst of failed logins on EXT-VPN-12 for the account wen.novak. The account failed 10 times in roughly 84 seconds and then authenticated successfully. The affected subnet is 10.203.163.0/24.

## Detection guidance

- Treat five or more failures against a single account inside a five-minute window, followed by a successful login, as a brute-force success.
- Required log fields: `account_id`, `event_type` and `timestamp`.
