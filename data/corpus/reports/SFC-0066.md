During the review of the incident on CITRIX‑GW‑16, the team observed a brief burst of failed authentication attempts that touched seven distinct accounts—including pchen and tara9—over a span of roughly 7 minutes. No successful logon followed, and the activity was logged under ticket INC‑64526, which was opened at 11:24 UTC and assigned to K. Sorensen.  

The detection logic that surfaced the event is deliberately scoped to flag a source host once failed logins span "at least 6" distinct accounts within "10 minutes". The rule explicitly requires the fields account_id, event_type, timestamp and source_host to be present; auth_method is noted as incidental and is not used as a condition. Repeated failures against a single account are excluded from the count, ensuring the signal focuses on the shallow, wide pattern that characterises password‑spraying activity. This approach allowed the SOC to surface the anomaly without attributing blame to any individual component.
