# Password Spraying From MAIL-GW-19

Summary

Authentication logs show one gateway, MAIL-GW-19, being used to try a small set of passwords across the directory. 5 distinct accounts were tried, mostly once each, over about 7 minutes; among them priya4, hiro.chen and mei.abdi. The affected subnet is 10.137.2.0/24.

Detection follow-up

Alert when at least 3 distinct accounts fail to authenticate from the same source host within 30 minutes. Required log fields: `account_id`, `event_type`, `timestamp` and `source_host`. For scoping, `auth_method` is not relevant to the behaviour and should be left out of the rule.
