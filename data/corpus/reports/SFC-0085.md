# Possible Credential Sharing on tara7

Summary

The account tara7 appeared to hold live sessions from different machines at nearly the same time. Successful sign-ins for tara7 came from JUMP-13 and then BASTION-16, about 6 minutes apart. Earlier the same day the account had 5 unremarkable successful logins.

Detection follow-up

Alert when the same account has successful logins from 2 or more different hosts within 15 minutes. To evaluate this, the log has to provide the username, the success/failure status of each event, event timestamps and the source workstation or gateway. In this case how the account authenticated varied between events and is not part of the pattern, so the rule must not depend on it.
