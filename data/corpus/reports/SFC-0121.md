# Provisioning Mismatch on svc-index21

- **Report ID:** SFC-0121
- **Source:** Synthetic corpus report (family F-B4-001)
- **ATT&CK Technique:** T1078.003 (Valid Accounts: Local Accounts)

## Narrative

The service account svc-index21 is provisioned for token authentication only, per the identity export. A successful login for svc-index21 was recorded on CITRIX-GW-04 using certificate instead. The affected subnet is 10.77.52.0/24.

## Analyst-confirmed detection parameters

- Flag successful logins that do not use the account's expected authentication method.
- The detection depends on the account identifier, the login result, the authentication method and the expected authentication method from the policy export.
- In this case whether multi-factor authentication was presented varied between events and is not part of the pattern, so the rule must not depend on it.
