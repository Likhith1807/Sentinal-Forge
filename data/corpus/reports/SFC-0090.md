**Runbook – Detect Concurrent Sessions for ulindqvist (Report SFC‑0089)**  

**Trigger** – An alert fires when the same account logs on successfully from *no fewer than two* distinct hosts within *thirty minutes*.

**Required fields** – Collect *the username*, *the login result*, *event timestamps* and *the source workstation or gateway* for each login event.  

**Step One:** Query the authentication logs for entries where *the username* equals **ulindqvist** and *the login result* is “Success”.  

**Step Two:** Group the results by *the source workstation or gateway* and sort by *event timestamps*. Identify any pair of successful logins that occur within *thirty minutes* of each other and originate from different hosts (e.g., JUMP‑11 then SSO‑GW‑12).  

**Step Three:** If such a pair is found, generate an incident ticket and note that *how the account authenticated* is incidental – do not use authentication method as a filter.  

**Scope** – Apply only to the subnet 10.147.25.0/24 as defined in the source report.  

**Remediation** – Review the flagged sessions, verify legitimate activity, and enforce multi‑factor authentication or session restrictions as needed.
