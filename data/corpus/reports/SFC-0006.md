During the review of the incident on RDP-EDGE-19, the team observed that the account zoe6 experienced a rapid series of failed login attempts followed by a successful authentication. The pattern began with nine consecutive failures occurring within roughly forty‑one seconds, after which the account was finally authenticated. Earlier that day the same account had five successful logins that did not raise any alerts. The detection logic is designed to fire when a single account accumulates "6 or more" failed logins inside a "90-second" window and then logs in successfully. To make this determination, the log must contain "the account being authenticated", "whether each attempt succeeded or failed", and "event timestamps". By ensuring these data elements are reliably captured, the rule can surface similar bursts without attributing fault to any individual or system component.
