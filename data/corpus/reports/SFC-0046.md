In the post‑incident review we note that a pattern of failed logins was observed on CITRIX‑GW‑16. Over a span of about 7 minutes, 8 distinct accounts—including tchen, rvargas and nadia.haddad—were each attempted once, triggering ticket INC‑58403 at 03:02 UTC, which was assigned to K. Sorensen. The detection rule is designed to flag a source host when failed logins span "6 or more" distinct accounts within "30 minutes". The rule deliberately ignores repeated failures on a single account. To evaluate the rule, the system must collect "the account identifier", "the login result", "event timestamps" and "the host the request originated from". No individual user was singled out for blame; the activity reflects a broad credential‑guessing attempt that matched the rule’s criteria, prompting the alert and subsequent investigation.
