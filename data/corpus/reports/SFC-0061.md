# Distributed Failed Logins Seen at JUMP-07

Summary

The SOC noticed a wide, shallow pattern of failures originating at JUMP-07. 8 distinct accounts were tried, mostly once each, over about 22 minutes; among them olindqvist, ulindqvist and gosei. Ticket INC-48681 was opened at 01:58 UTC and assigned to R. Ibarra.

Detection follow-up

Alert when no fewer than 5 distinct accounts fail to authenticate from the same source host within 15 minutes. Required log fields: `account_id`, `event_type`, `timestamp` and `source_host`. For scoping, `auth_method` is not relevant to the behaviour and should be left out of the rule.
