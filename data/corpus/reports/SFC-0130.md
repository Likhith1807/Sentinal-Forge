**Runbook – Service Account svc‑deploy06 Used an Unexpected Method (Report ID: SFC‑0129)**  

**Purpose:** Detect and respond when the service account svc‑deploy06 authenticates with a method other than its certificate‑based “expected_auth_method”.

**Trigger:** A successful login for svc‑deploy06 recorded on WIN‑LAB‑02 using a password instead of the certificate.

**Required fields:** “account_id”, “event_type”, “auth_method”, “expected_auth_method”.

**Steps**  
1. Query the authentication logs for entries where “account_id” equals **svc‑deploy06** and “event_type” is **login_success**.  
2. Filter those entries to isolate records where “auth_method” does **not** match the “expected_auth_method” (certificate).  
3. Verify the source host is WIN‑LAB‑02 and that the source IP belongs to subnet 10.212.83.0/24.  
4. If a mismatch is found, create an incident ticket referencing Report ID SFC‑0129 and tag the ATT&CK technique T1078.003.  
5. Notify the privileged‑access team, revoke the password‑based credential for svc‑deploy06, and enforce certificate‑only authentication.  
6. Document the finding, update the account’s authentication policy, and close the ticket after confirming remediation.
