RDP-EDGE-07 logged a burst of failed RDP sign‑ins targeting six distinct accounts—including gchen, liam.duarte and viktor.chen—across the 10.211.164.0/24 subnet in roughly seven minutes. The pattern aligns with ATT&CK technique T1110.003 (Password Spraying) and is captured under Report ID SFC-0073. Analysts should flag any instance where “4 or more” distinct accounts generate failures from a single host within a “1-hour” window as a probable spray attempt.

Effective detection hinges on collecting the fields “account_id”, “event_type”, “timestamp” and “source_host”. While “auth_method” appears in logs, it does not influence this behavior and should be omitted from the rule set. This concise data set enables rapid identification of credential‑guessing campaigns without unnecessary noise.
