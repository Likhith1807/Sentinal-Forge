# Credential Guessing Across Many Accounts via EXT-VPN-09

- **Report ID:** SFC-0049
- **Source:** Synthetic corpus report (family F-B2-005)
- **ATT&CK Technique:** T1110.003 (Password Spraying)

## Narrative

Authentication logs show one gateway, EXT-VPN-09, being used to try a small set of passwords across the directory. 6 distinct accounts were tried, mostly once each, over about 14 minutes; among them amir3, uma.abdi and hiro4. Ticket INC-49523 was opened at 07:31 UTC and assigned to R. Ibarra.

## Detection guidance

- Flag a source host once failed logins span 4+ distinct accounts within five minutes; repeated failures on a single account do not count.
- The rule reads `account_id`, `event_type`, `timestamp` and `source_host` from the authentication log.
- In this case `auth_method` varied between events and is not part of the pattern, so the rule must not depend on it.
