**Runbook: Detect Possible Credential Sharing**

1. Flag any account that holds live sessions on **"2 or more"** distinct hosts inside a **"30-minute"** window.  
2. Retrieve logs for **"the account being authenticated"** (e.g., bvargas) and capture **"the success/failure status of each event"**.  
3. Extract **"event timestamps"** and **"the originating host"** for every authentication attempt.  
4. Correlate the timestamps: if two successful logins (e.g., one from RDP-EDGE-04 and a second from JUMP-14) occur without an intervening logout and fall within the 30‑minute interval, treat this as a potential credential‑sharing incident.  
5. Open a ticket, document the findings, and notify the security response team for further analysis.
