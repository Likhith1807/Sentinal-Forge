In the aftermath of the credential‑guessing activity targeting svc‑sync21 on MAIL‑GW‑10, the incident review notes that a cluster of failed login attempts preceded a successful authentication. The pattern that triggered the alert required "no fewer than 3" failed logins on the same account within "2 minutes", followed by a success. The detection logic relied on three core fields: "account_id", "event_type" and "timestamp". Although the logs also contain an "auth_method" attribute, it was treated as incidental and not used to fire the rule. The burst consisted of six rejections over an 84‑second window before the account finally logged in from the 10.164.234.0/24 subnet. By focusing on the defined criteria and the essential fields, the team was able to surface the event without attributing blame, and the review highlights the importance of maintaining the same data requirements for future monitoring.
