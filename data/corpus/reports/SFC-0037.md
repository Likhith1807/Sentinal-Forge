# Brute-Force Pattern on SSO-GW-17

Summary

The identity team reported a burst of failed logins on SSO-GW-17 for the account quinn7. The account failed 10 times in roughly 96 seconds and then authenticated successfully. Ticket INC-28383 was opened at 13:02 UTC and assigned to R. Ibarra.

Detection follow-up

Flag any account that fails to authenticate 6 or more times within 5 minutes and subsequently succeeds. The rule reads `account_id`, `event_type` and `timestamp` from the authentication log. For scoping, `auth_method` is not relevant to the behaviour and should be left out of the rule.
