# Sign-In Without a Second Factor: bianca.brennan

Summary

bianca.brennan is required to use multi-factor authentication by policy. bianca.brennan logged in from BASTION-14 and the event records that MFA was not used. Ticket INC-68296 was opened at 23:03 UTC and assigned to S. Whitfield.

Detection follow-up

Flag any successful login lacking MFA on an MFA-required account. The rule reads `account_id`, `event_type`, `mfa_used` and `mfa_required` from the authentication log. For scoping, `auth_method` is not relevant to the behaviour and should be left out of the rule.
