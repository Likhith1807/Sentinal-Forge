Endpoint telemetry from EXT‑VPN‑13 showed PowerShell launching with an encoded command. The parent process was a document reader, and the command line stretched to 514 characters. The incident was logged under ticket INC‑30551 at 15:07 UTC and assigned to M. Delacroix. In response, the detection logic was clarified: an alert should fire whenever powershell.exe starts with an encoded command argument. To evaluate that rule, the system must capture both “the process name” and “the full command line”. This ensures that any future occurrence of a similarly encoded PowerShell invocation can be identified promptly, allowing the security team to investigate without assigning blame.
