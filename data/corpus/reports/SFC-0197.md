# Sign-In Without a Second Factor: ines2

Summary

The policy export marks ines2 as MFA-required. ines2 logged in from SSO-GW-05 and the event records that MFA was not used. The affected subnet is 10.113.14.0/24.

Detection follow-up

Flag any successful login lacking MFA on an MFA-required account. To evaluate this, the log has to provide the account identifier, the sign-in outcome, whether a second factor was used and whether policy requires MFA for the account.
