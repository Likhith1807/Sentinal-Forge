Ticket INC-73391 reports a medium‑severity event observed on gateway RDP-EDGE-04 where an attacker attempted password guesses against eight distinct accounts, including hiro.abdi, giyer and xnakamura, within roughly four minutes. The pattern of interest is any source host that generates failed logins against "6 or more" different "account_id"s in a rolling "30 minutes" window; isolated repeat failures on a single account are ignored.  

To surface this activity, a rule must ingest the fields "account_id", "event_type", "timestamp" and "source_host" from the authentication log. The "auth_method" field is explicitly excluded, as it does not influence the behavior. The rule is scoped to the 10.19.247.0/24 subnet.
