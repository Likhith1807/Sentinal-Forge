# Suspected DNS Tunnelling From BASTION-02

- **Report ID:** SFC-0201
- **Source:** Synthetic corpus report (family F-U-001)
- **ATT&CK Technique:** T1071.004 (Application Layer Protocol: DNS)

## Narrative

BASTION-02 generated an unusual volume of long DNS lookups to a single domain. Analysts saw 376 queries with names over 50 characters in a short period. Ticket INC-91695 was opened at 03:39 UTC and assigned to R. Ibarra.

## Detection guidance

- Alert when one host issues more than 100 DNS queries with names longer than 50 characters within 30 minutes.
- Required log fields: `dns_query` and `query_length`.
