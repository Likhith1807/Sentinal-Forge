# Provisioning Mismatch on svc-batch03

Summary

The service account svc-batch03 is provisioned for certificate authentication only, per the identity export. A successful login for svc-batch03 was recorded on MAIL-GW-13 using password instead. Earlier the same day the account had 3 unremarkable successful logins.

Detection follow-up

Alert on any successful login where the authentication method differs from the method the account is provisioned for; any direction of mismatch counts. The rule reads `account_id`, `event_type`, `auth_method` and `expected_auth_method` from the authentication log.
