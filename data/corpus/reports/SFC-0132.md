Subject: Detection Request – Mismatch Between Provisioned and Used Auth Method (INC-30546)

Hi Detection‑Engineering,

I’m opening a ticket (INC-30546, severity: high) to get a rule built for the scenario we saw on EXT‑VPN‑07. The service account **svc‑batch18** logged in successfully with a token, even though its provisioning lists a certificate as the only approved method.

The detection should fire on any successful login where the authentication method differs from the method the account is provisioned for, regardless of direction. The rule needs to pull the fields **"account_id"**, **"event_type"**, **"auth_method"** and **"expected_auth_method"** from the authentication log. Please note that **"mfa_used"** varied between events in this case and must not be part of the matching logic.

For context, earlier the same day the account had two unremarkable successful logins that matched its provisioning. Let me know if you need any clarification.
