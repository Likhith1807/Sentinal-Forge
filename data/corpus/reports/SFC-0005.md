# Brute-Force Pattern on RDP-EDGE-19

Summary

The identity team reported a burst of failed logins on RDP-EDGE-19 for the account zoe6. The account failed 9 times in roughly 41 seconds and then authenticated successfully. Earlier the same day the account had 5 unremarkable successful logins.

Detection follow-up

The rule should fire once one account accumulates 6 or more failed logins inside a 90-second window and then logs in successfully. To evaluate this, the log has to provide the account being authenticated, whether each attempt succeeded or failed and event timestamps.
