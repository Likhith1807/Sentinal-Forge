# Sign-In Without a Second Factor: bianca6

Summary

The policy export marks bianca6 as MFA-required. A successful sign-in from BASTION-10 showed no second factor. The affected subnet is 10.110.71.0/24.

Detection follow-up

Alert when a successful login is recorded without MFA for an account whose policy requires MFA. To evaluate this, the log has to provide the account name, the login result, whether a second factor was used and the account's MFA requirement in the policy.
