**Runbook – Detect Unexpected Authentication Method**  
Ticket INC-43612 | Severity high | Analyst K. Sorensen  

**Trigger** – A successful login where the used method differs from the account’s provisioning (e.g., svc‑backup05 on EXT‑VPN‑11 signing in with a password while the certificate is the sole approved method).

**Required data fields** – Collect “account_id”, “event_type”, “auth_method” and “expected_auth_method” from the log source. The field “mfa_used” may appear but must be ignored.

**Steps**  
1. **First**, query the authentication logs for events where “event_type” equals a successful login.  
2. **Second**, filter results by matching each record’s “account_id” to its “expected_auth_method”.  
3. **Third**, flag any record where “auth_method” does not equal the “expected_auth_method”.  
4. **Fourth**, generate an alert that includes the offending “account_id”, the observed “auth_method”, and the “expected_auth_method”.  
5. **Fifth**, verify that the alert does not consider the value of “mfa_used”, as it is not part of the detection logic.  

**Note** – The rule should tolerate normal activity; earlier the same day the account logged in successfully five times without issue.
