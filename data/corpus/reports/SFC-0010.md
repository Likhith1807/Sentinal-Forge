**Runbook – Credential‑Guessing Detection for svc‑deploy04 (Report SFC‑0009)**  

**Trigger** – Alert when “5 or more” failed sign‑ins for the same account occur within “2 minutes”, followed by a successful sign‑in.  

**Required data** – Capture “the username”, “whether each attempt succeeded or failed” and “event timestamps”. “The client IP” is explicitly excluded from the rule.  

**Steps**  

**Step A** – Pull authentication logs from SSO‑GW‑06. Filter for entries that reference svc‑deploy04.  

**Step B** – Group records by “the username”. For each group, examine “event timestamps” to identify any window of “2 minutes” that contains “5 or more” failures.  

**Step C** – Within the same group, check the next record after the failure window for a successful sign‑in.  

**Step D** – If the pattern is found, generate an alert referencing ATT&CK techniques T1110 (Brute Force) and T1078 (Valid Accounts).  

**Step E** – Document the incident, noting the preceding 9 rejected attempts over 73 seconds and the later successful login, and forward to the response team.
