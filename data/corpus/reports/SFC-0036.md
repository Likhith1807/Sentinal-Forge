In the review of ticket INC-50582, the team observed that the account amir4 experienced a series of failed login attempts followed by a successful authentication on SSO-GW-07. The pattern consisted of 6 consecutive failures over roughly 84 seconds before the eventual success. Our detection logic is designed to flag any account that fails to authenticate "five or more" times within "ten minutes" and then succeeds. To evaluate this condition we rely on "the username", "whether each attempt succeeded or failed", and "event timestamps". By correlating these data points, the system correctly identified the activity without attributing blame. The investigation also noted that the source resides in the 10.66.100.0/24 subnet, which was recorded for context but not part of the trigger criteria. The review highlights the importance of consistent log collection and the value of the defined detection parameters.
