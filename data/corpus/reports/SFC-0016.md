During the handoff for INC-42863, the on‑call analyst noted that the authentication logs from EXT‑VPN‑06 showed a series of failed attempts targeting the tokafor account. The account experienced 14 failures in roughly 47 seconds before a successful login. The incident prompted a review of the detection criteria: any account that fails to authenticate **more than nine** times within **90 seconds** and then succeeds should be flagged. To support this rule, the log must contain the fields `account_id`, `event_type` and `timestamp`. Although the `source_ip` value changed between each event, it is not part of the pattern and therefore must not be required for the alert. The team documented the requirement without assigning blame, focusing on the need for consistent log collection and the precise definition of the failure‑then‑success window to improve future detection.
