# Password Spraying From CITRIX-GW-16

- **Report ID:** SFC-0065
- **Source:** Synthetic corpus report (family F-B2-013)
- **ATT&CK Technique:** T1110.003 (Password Spraying)

## Narrative

The SOC noticed a wide, shallow pattern of failures originating at CITRIX-GW-16. Over roughly 7 minutes the host touched 7 different accounts, including pchen and tara9; none of them went on to a successful login. Ticket INC-64526 was opened at 11:24 UTC and assigned to K. Sorensen.

## Analyst-confirmed detection parameters

- Flag a source host once failed logins span at least 6 distinct accounts within 10 minutes; repeated failures on a single account do not count.
- Fields needed: `account_id`, `event_type`, `timestamp` and `source_host`.
- Note that `auth_method` is incidental here; do not condition the detection on it.
