Ticket INC-34886, flagged as high severity and analyzed by S. Whitfield, documents that the account uma7 accessed the network from host JUMP-07 and the event log shows a successful login where MFA was not used despite the policy marking the account as MFA‑required. The core trigger for a detection rule is any successful login that lacks MFA for an account whose policy mandates MFA, as demonstrated by this incident.

The rule must ingest the fields "account_id", "event_type", "mfa_used" and "mfa_required" to evaluate the condition; the variable "auth_method" observed across events is explicitly excluded from the pattern. The brief notes that earlier the same day the account recorded five unremarkable successful logins, underscoring that the anomaly is the absence of MFA when it is required.
