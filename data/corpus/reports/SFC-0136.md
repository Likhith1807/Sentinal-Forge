During the handoff for INC-47302, the team noted that the service account svc‑batch26 is required by policy to authenticate exclusively with a token. The detection logic was triggered when a successful login for svc‑batch26 was observed on EXT‑VPN‑14 using a password instead of the token. The rule that raised the alert looks for any successful authentication event where the method employed does not match the method defined for the account. To evaluate each event, the rule inspects the fields “account_id”, “event_type”, “auth_method” and “expected_auth_method”. Although the logs also contain a “mfa_used” flag that varied between events, the pattern deliberately ignores that field, ensuring the rule remains focused on the mismatch between the actual and expected authentication methods. This approach isolates the deviation without attributing blame, allowing the team to address the configuration gap.
