# Interactive Authentication by svc-sync04

Summary

The service account svc-sync04 is provisioned for token authentication only, per the identity export. On MAIL-GW-10, svc-sync04 signed in successfully with certificate, which does not match its provisioning. The affected subnet is 10.65.48.0/24.

Detection follow-up

Flag successful logins that do not use the account's expected authentication method. Required log fields: `account_id`, `event_type`, `auth_method` and `expected_auth_method`. Note that `mfa_used` is incidental here; do not condition the detection on it.
