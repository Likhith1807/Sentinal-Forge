# Credential Guessing Across Many Accounts via CITRIX-GW-16

Summary

The SOC noticed a wide, shallow pattern of failures originating at CITRIX-GW-16. 8 distinct accounts were tried, mostly once each, over about 7 minutes; among them tchen, rvargas and nadia.haddad. Ticket INC-58403 was opened at 03:02 UTC and assigned to K. Sorensen.

Detection follow-up

Flag a source host once failed logins span 6 or more distinct accounts within 30 minutes; repeated failures on a single account do not count. Evaluating the rule requires the account identifier, the login result, event timestamps and the host the request originated from.
