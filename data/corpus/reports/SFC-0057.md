# Credential Guessing Across Many Accounts via MAIL-GW-10

- **Report ID:** SFC-0057
- **Source:** Synthetic corpus report (family F-B2-009)
- **ATT&CK Technique:** T1110.003 (Password Spraying)

## Narrative

The SOC noticed a wide, shallow pattern of failures originating at MAIL-GW-10. Over roughly 12 minutes the host touched 12 different accounts, including umoreau and farid4; none of them went on to a successful login. The affected subnet is 10.170.227.0/24.

## Recommended detection

- Flag a source host once failed logins span 8 or more distinct accounts within 30 minutes; repeated failures on a single account do not count.
- The detection depends on the account name, whether each attempt succeeded or failed, when each event occurred and the machine each attempt came from.
