The post‑incident review notes that the service account svc-index05 is configured by policy to authenticate exclusively with a certificate. On the host WIN‑LAB‑17 the account completed a successful sign‑in using a password, which diverged from its provisioning. Earlier that day the same account recorded five routine successful logins that matched the expected method, so the anomaly was isolated to the password‑based event.

The detection logic is designed to trigger when a successful authentication presents a method that differs from the one defined for the account. To evaluate each sign‑in, the rule extracts four fields from the authentication log: "account_id", "event_type", "auth_method" and "expected_auth_method". By comparing the observed "auth_method" with the "expected_auth_method" for the given "account_id", the rule can flag the deviation without attributing fault, enabling a timely investigation of the unexpected credential use.
