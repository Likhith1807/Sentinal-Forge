# Distributed Failed Logins Seen at CITRIX-GW-03

Summary

The SOC noticed a wide, shallow pattern of failures originating at CITRIX-GW-03. Over roughly 12 minutes the host touched 8 different accounts, including zosei and hiro.chen; none of them went on to a successful login. The affected subnet is 10.158.10.0/24.

Detection follow-up

Alert when at least six distinct accounts fail to authenticate from the same source host within ten minutes. To evaluate this, the log has to provide the username, the login result, the event time and the host the request originated from.
