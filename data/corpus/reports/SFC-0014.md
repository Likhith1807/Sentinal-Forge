An adversary targeted the account tara9 from the host JUMP-12, issuing a rapid series of rejected password attempts—9 failures in roughly 41 seconds—before establishing a successful session. The incident was logged under ticket INC-96345 at 18:47 UTC and assigned to M. Delacroix for investigation.

The detection logic flags activity as a brute‑force success when it observes no fewer than 6 failures against a single account within a 3-minute window that are followed by a successful login. Implementing the rule requires capturing the account name, the sign-in outcome and when each event occurred to reliably surface this pattern.
