# Possible Credential Sharing on bvargas

Summary

bvargas logged in from two places without any logout in between. bvargas authenticated at RDP-EDGE-04; roughly 8 minutes later a second success arrived from JUMP-14. Earlier the same day the account had 7 unremarkable successful logins.

Detection follow-up

The rule should fire if one account holds live sessions on 2 or more distinct hosts inside a 30-minute window. Evaluating the rule requires the account being authenticated, the success/failure status of each event, event timestamps and the originating host.
