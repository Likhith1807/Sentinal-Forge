Subject: Detection rule request – distributed failed logins from JUMP-07  

Team,  

During routine monitoring we observed a shallow but wide set of authentication failures originating from **JUMP-07**. Eight distinct accounts were targeted, most only once, over roughly 22 minutes. For scoping purposes, **auth_method** is not relevant to the pattern and should be omitted from the rule logic.
