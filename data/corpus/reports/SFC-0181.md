# MFA Bypass on a Protected Account

Summary

wen.tanaka is required to use multi-factor authentication by policy. wen.tanaka logged in from WIN-LAB-08 and the event records that MFA was not used. Ticket INC-87768 was opened at 15:47 UTC and assigned to M. Delacroix.

Detection follow-up

The rule should fire on a successful sign-in with no second factor when the account's policy mandates it. The rule reads `account_id`, `event_type`, `mfa_used` and `mfa_required` from the authentication log.
