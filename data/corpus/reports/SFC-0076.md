Ticket INC‑68997 | Severity medium | Analyst M. Delacroix  

In the aftermath of the event on CITRIX‑GW‑08, the team reviewed the pattern of failed sign‑ins without assigning blame. The logs showed five distinct accounts—among them wduarte, wkowalski and kavya.abdi—each attempted roughly once over a span of about four minutes. The detection rule that would have raised an alert required that failed logins span "no fewer than 3" distinct accounts within "15 minutes", while ignoring repeated failures on a single account. To evaluate that rule, the investigation confirmed that the log entries contain "the username", "the login result", "the event time" and "the originating host". With those fields present, the rule could correctly flag the source host responsible for the spread of failures. The affected subnet was identified as 10.103.3.0/24, providing context for any network‑level follow‑up. This review highlights the data prerequisites and timing logic that underpin the detection without attributing fault to any individual.
