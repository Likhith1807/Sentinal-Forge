During the handoff for INC-27133, the team noted that the user ximena3 logged in from two distinct gateways without an intervening logout. The first successful authentication occurred on CITRIX‑GW‑17, and roughly 7 minutes later a second success was recorded on JUMP‑16. In response, the detection logic was refined to treat successful sign‑ins for a single account on “2 or more” distinct hosts within a “15-minute” window as a possible indicator of credential sharing. The rule extracts the fields account_id, event_type, timestamp and source_host from the authentication log. Although the auth_method varied between the two events, it is explicitly excluded from the pattern, ensuring the rule does not depend on that attribute. This adjustment aligns the monitoring approach with the observed behavior while maintaining a focus on the relevant data elements.
