The investigation began when the monitoring system flagged a successful sign‑in that lacked a second factor for an account that the policy export marks as MFA‑required. The login originated from the host JUMP‑18 under the user name nadia.vargas, and the event record indicated that no multi‑factor authentication was applied. The alert triggered ticket INC‑24712 at 08:33 UTC, which was then assigned to L. Adeyemi for follow‑up.

To detect this condition, the rule must examine four fields: the identifier of the user, captured as "account_id", the kind of activity, captured as "event_type", and the boolean flags "mfa_used" and "mfa_required". The attribute "auth_method" is intentionally omitted from the rule because it does not affect the detection of a missing second factor on an MFA‑required account. This approach ensures that any future occurrence will be surfaced promptly without attributing blame.
