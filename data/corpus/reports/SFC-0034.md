The identity team observed a rapid burst of failed RDP logins on RDP-EDGE-05 targeting the account svc-notify18. Twelve failures occurred in roughly 58 seconds before a successful authentication was recorded. The incident generated ticket INC-69710 at 22:06 UTC, which was assigned to L. Adeyemi.  

Analysts should trigger an alert when no fewer than 10 failed sign‑ins for the same account occur within 5 minutes and are followed by a successful sign‑in. Evaluating this rule requires the account name, the success/failure status of each event and the time of each attempt.
