**Runbook – Brute‑Force Success Detection (INC-92619)**  

5+ – Verify that there are at least 5+ failed sign‑in attempts for a single account on SSO‑GW‑07 within a short time window, followed by a successful login.  

60-second – Ensure the failed attempts occur within a 60-second interval before the success.  

the username – Extract the username (e.g., svc‑monitor25) from each log entry to correlate the attempts.  

the sign-in outcome – Review the sign‑in outcome field to differentiate failures from the succeeding login.  

the time of each attempt – Use the time of each attempt to confirm the sequence fits the 5+ / 60‑second pattern.  

the client IP – Exclude the client IP from the rule, as it is not relevant to this behaviour.
