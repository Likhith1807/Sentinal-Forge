INC-24046 was handed off from K. Sorensen to the next shift after BASTION-18 logged a burst of failed sign‑ins against a range of user accounts. In roughly 4 minutes, eight distinct accounts—including bianca.nakamura, omar.lindqvist and elif4—were each hit with a single failure, indicating a rapid, low‑volume probing attempt from the same host.

Analysts recommend a rule that fires when a single source host generates failed sign‑ins for "4 or more" different accounts inside a "10-minute" window. The rule must ingest the fields "account_id", "event_type", "timestamp" and "source_host". For scoping purposes, "auth_method" is not relevant and should be omitted from the detection logic. This pattern isolates the observed behavior without relying on authentication method details.
