Ticket INC-45709, severity medium, was opened by analyst T. Nguyen after the account mmoreau was observed holding live sessions from different machines at nearly the same time. The logs showed a successful sign‑in from CITRIX‑GW‑09 followed by another from BASTION‑05 about 7 minutes later. The incident prompted a request to flag concurrent use: successful authentication for one account from no fewer than two separate machines within fifteen minutes. The detection logic relies on four fields: account_id, event_type, timestamp and source_host. Although source_ip appears in the raw data, it is incidental and should not be used as a condition. The review also notes that earlier the same day the account had 2 unremarkable successful logins, which did not trigger the rule. No blame is assigned; the focus is on refining the rule to capture the intended pattern while respecting the data constraints.
