# Sign-In Without a Second Factor: zoe.petrov

- **Report ID:** SFC-0193
- **Source:** Synthetic corpus report (family F-B5-017)
- **ATT&CK Technique:** T1556 (Modify Authentication Process), T1621 (MFA Request Generation)

## Narrative

zoe.petrov is required to use multi-factor authentication by policy. A successful sign-in from RDP-EDGE-19 showed no second factor. Earlier the same day the account had 5 unremarkable successful logins.

## Detection guidance

- Alert when a successful login is recorded without MFA for an account whose policy requires MFA.
- The rule reads `account_id`, `event_type`, `mfa_used` and `mfa_required` from the authentication log.
- Note that `auth_method` is incidental here; do not condition the detection on it.
