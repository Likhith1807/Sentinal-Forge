A recent handoff on INC-68843 highlighted a policy breach involving the user viktor.mensah. The security policy mandates a second factor for this account, yet a successful sign‑in from RDP-EDGE-15 was recorded without any multi‑factor challenge. Analysts are instructed to generate an alert whenever a successful login occurs without MFA for any account whose policy requires MFA, mirroring the scenario observed in this case.

The detection logic hinges on four data points: the account identifier, the success/failure status of each event, whether multi-factor authentication was presented and whether policy requires MFA for the account. Although the credential type used is incidental here, it must not be used as a condition in the rule.
