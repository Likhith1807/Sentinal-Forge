# Mass File Renames Consistent With Ransomware on SSO-GW-16

- **Report ID:** SFC-0209
- **Source:** Synthetic corpus report (family F-U-005)
- **ATT&CK Technique:** T1486 (Data Encrypted for Impact)

## Narrative

File-server auditing showed many files renamed with a new extension on SSO-GW-16. In the observed burst, 685 files were renamed within a few minutes. Ticket INC-61249 was opened at 15:54 UTC and assigned to K. Sorensen.

## Recommended detection

- Alert when more than 50 files are renamed to a new extension within 10 minutes on one host.
- Required log fields: `file_path` and `file_extension`.
