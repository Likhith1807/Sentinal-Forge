# Low-and-Slow Spray Against the Directory

- **Report ID:** SFC-0041
- **Source:** Synthetic corpus report (family F-B2-001)
- **ATT&CK Technique:** T1110.003 (Password Spraying)

## Narrative

The SOC noticed a wide, shallow pattern of failures originating at JUMP-03. Over roughly 9 minutes the host touched 5 different accounts, including mei.osei and priya.lindqvist; none of them went on to a successful login. Ticket INC-50233 was opened at 07:03 UTC and assigned to P. Banerjee.

## Analyst-confirmed detection parameters

- Flag a source host once failed logins span 3 or more distinct accounts within 5 minutes; repeated failures on a single account do not count.
- The detection depends on the username, whether each attempt succeeded or failed, event timestamps and the originating host.
