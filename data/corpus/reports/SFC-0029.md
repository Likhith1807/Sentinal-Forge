# Repeated Failed Logins Followed by Success on ytanaka

Summary

ytanaka was targeted through EXT-VPN-17: a run of rejected password attempts was followed by a successful session. After 15 rejected attempts spread across 73 seconds, ytanaka logged in. Earlier the same day the account had 3 unremarkable successful logins.

Detection follow-up

Detection guidance: raise an alert when 10 or more failed sign-ins for the same account occur within 90 seconds, followed by a successful sign-in for that account. Required log fields: `account_id`, `event_type` and `timestamp`.
