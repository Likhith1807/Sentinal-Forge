The review notes that the service account svc-archive09 is configured with a password as its sole approved authentication method. A successful login was observed on BASTION-14 using a token instead, prompting the opening of ticket INC-91463 at 07:29 UTC and its assignment to K. Sorensen. The detection logic is intentionally simple: it alerts on any successful login where the authentication method differs from the method the account is provisioned for, regardless of direction of the mismatch. The rule extracts the fields `"account_id"`, `"event_type"`, `"auth_method"` and `"expected_auth_method"` from the authentication log to make this comparison. By focusing on the mismatch rather than the individual user or system, the alert surface remains consistent and actionable, allowing the response team to investigate the deviation without attributing blame. The incident underscores the value of clear provisioning data and automated checks that surface unexpected authentication paths promptly.
