# Credential Guessing Across Many Accounts via RDP-EDGE-07

- **Report ID:** SFC-0073
- **Source:** Synthetic corpus report (family F-B2-017)
- **ATT&CK Technique:** T1110.003 (Password Spraying)

## Narrative

RDP-EDGE-07 recorded failed sign-ins against many different accounts in a short period. 6 distinct accounts were tried, mostly once each, over about 7 minutes; among them gchen, liam.duarte and viktor.chen. The affected subnet is 10.211.164.0/24.

## Recommended detection

- Treat 4 or more distinct accounts failing from one host within a 1-hour window as password spraying.
- Fields needed: `account_id`, `event_type`, `timestamp` and `source_host`.
- For scoping, `auth_method` is not relevant to the behaviour and should be left out of the rule.
