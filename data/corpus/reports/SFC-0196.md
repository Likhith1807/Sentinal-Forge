Ticket INC-97983 was opened with a low severity rating and assigned to analyst M. Delacroix. The incident stemmed from a policy export that incorrectly marked the user amir.vargas as “MFA‑required.” When a login was processed through CITRIX‑GW‑18, the authentication succeeded even though no second factor was presented. The detection rule that should have fired is intended to trigger on any successful sign‑in where the account’s policy requires multi‑factor authentication but the log shows none. To evaluate this condition the rule reads the fields “account_id,” “event_type,” “mfa_used” and “mfa_required” from the authentication log. Although the “auth_method” value differed between events, it is explicitly excluded from the pattern, ensuring the rule does not depend on it. The affected subnet was 10.227.76.0/24.
