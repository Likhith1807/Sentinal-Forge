Ticket INC-24936 (Severity: high) assigned to Analyst K. Sorensen documents a credential‑guessing pattern observed in the CITRIX-GW-18 logs targeting the uma4 account. The account recorded six failed attempts within roughly 58 seconds before a successful authentication, prompting a rule that should fire when an account accumulates "5 or more" failed logins inside a "5-minute" window and then logs in successfully.  

The detection logic requires the fields "account_id", "event_type" and "timestamp" from the authentication stream. Although the log includes an "auth_method" attribute, it is incidental and must not be used as a condition. The same day the uma4 account also generated six unremarkable successful logins, which are not part of the trigger.
