# Brute-Force Pattern on EXT-VPN-09

Summary

svc-notify05 was targeted through EXT-VPN-09: a run of rejected password attempts was followed by a successful session. After 5 rejected attempts spread across 41 seconds, svc-notify05 logged in. Ticket INC-90021 was opened at 07:06 UTC and assigned to P. Banerjee.

Detection follow-up

The rule should fire once one account accumulates 4 or more failed logins inside a 2-minute window and then logs in successfully. To evaluate this, the log has to provide the account name, the success/failure status of each event and when each event occurred. Note that the originating IP is incidental here; do not condition the detection on it.
