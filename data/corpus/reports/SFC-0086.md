During the review of the tara7 activity, the team observed that the account maintained live sessions from distinct machines in close succession. Successful sign‑ins were recorded from JUMP‑13 and then BASTION‑16, with only a short interval between them. Earlier that day the account had five unremarkable successful logins, which did not raise concern. The detection logic is designed to fire when the same account has successful logins from "2 or more" different hosts within "15 minutes". To evaluate such a condition the log must contain "the username", "the success/failure status of each event", "event timestamps" and "the source workstation or gateway". In this instance the way the account accessed the system varied, but that detail—captured by the phrase "how the account authenticated"—is not part of the pattern and therefore does not influence the alert. The review focuses on the data requirements and the trigger criteria, without assigning blame, and highlights that the observed behaviour aligns with the defined detection parameters.
