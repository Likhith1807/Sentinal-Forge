Analysts observed a credential‑use anomaly involving the service account svc‑sync12. The account, which according to policy must authenticate solely with a certificate, logged a successful sign‑in on MAIL‑GW‑19 using a token. The activity originated from the 10.157.81.0/24 subnet, indicating a deviation from the prescribed authentication profile.  

The detection rule is triggered when a successful sign‑in presents a mismatch between the observed "auth_method" and the configured "expected_auth_method". It evaluates the fields "account_id", "event_type", "auth_method" and "expected_auth_method" from the authentication log to flag the incident.
