Password‑spraying activity was observed on the MAIL‑GW‑19 gateway, where five distinct accounts—including priya4, hiro.chen and mei.abdi—were each targeted with a small password set. The attempts occurred over roughly 7 minutes from the 10.137.2.0/24 subnet, indicating a rapid, low‑volume credential‑guessing campaign against the directory.

The detection logic fires an alert when "at least 3" distinct accounts fail to authenticate from the same "source_host" within "30 minutes". The rule requires the log fields "account_id", "event_type", "timestamp" and "source_host" to be present, while "auth_method" is explicitly excluded as it does not influence the pattern. This configuration isolates the characteristic spray behavior without reliance on authentication method details.
