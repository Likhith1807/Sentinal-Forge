**Runbook: Alert on MFA‑bypass for accounts requiring MFA**  

1. When a login event is logged, capture **the username** from the record.  
2. Record **whether each attempt succeeded or failed** and **whether a second factor was used**.  
3. Query the policy store to determine **whether policy requires MFA for the account**.  
4. If the policy indicates MFA is required and the login succeeded **without a second factor**, generate an alert referencing the original ticket (e.g., INC-31507).  
5. Document the incident, noting that **how the account authenticated** is incidental and should not affect the detection logic.  

*Trigger:* A successful sign‑in from WIN‑LAB‑15 for the account *uma8* showed no second factor despite the MFA requirement. This runbook ensures the alert fires under those conditions.
