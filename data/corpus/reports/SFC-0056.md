During the handoff for INC-86123, the SOC observed a wide, shallow pattern of authentication failures that originated from MAIL-GW-19. Eight distinct accounts—including cbrennan, ymensah and omar.lindqvist—were each tried roughly once over a period of about seven minutes. The team agreed to treat “6 or more” distinct accounts failing from a single source within a “10-minute” window as indicative of password‑spraying activity. To apply this rule consistently, the log must contain “the account name”, “whether each attempt succeeded or failed”, “event timestamps” and “the originating host”. By confirming that these fields were present for each record, the analysts could reliably flag the activity without attributing blame. The review highlights that the detection logic was triggered appropriately and that the required data elements were available, supporting a clear, objective assessment of the incident.
