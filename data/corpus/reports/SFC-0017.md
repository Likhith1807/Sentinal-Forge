# Brute-Force Pattern on MAIL-GW-09

- **Report ID:** SFC-0017
- **Source:** Synthetic corpus report (family F-B1-009)
- **ATT&CK Technique:** T1110 (Brute Force), T1078 (Valid Accounts)

## Narrative

farid.brennan was targeted through MAIL-GW-09: a run of rejected password attempts was followed by a successful session. The account failed 15 times in roughly 84 seconds and then authenticated successfully. Earlier the same day the account had 3 unremarkable successful logins.

## Analyst-confirmed detection parameters

- Detection guidance: raise an alert when 10+ failed sign-ins for the same account occur within thirty seconds, followed by a successful sign-in for that account.
- To evaluate this, the log has to provide the username, the sign-in outcome and when each event occurred.
- In this case the authentication method varied between events and is not part of the pattern, so the rule must not depend on it.
