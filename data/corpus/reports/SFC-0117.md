# Possible Credential Sharing on ximena9

Summary

Access review flagged ximena9 for authenticating from more than one host in quick succession. Successful sign-ins for ximena9 came from EXT-VPN-16 and then WIN-LAB-19, about 7 minutes apart. The affected subnet is 10.59.55.0/24.

Detection follow-up

Treat successful sign-ins for a single account on at least 2 distinct hosts within a 15-minute window as possible credential sharing. The rule reads `account_id`, `event_type`, `timestamp` and `source_host` from the authentication log. Note that `source_ip` is incidental here; do not condition the detection on it.
